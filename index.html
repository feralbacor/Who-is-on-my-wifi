What the severities mean

critical
An unknown device is online with a weak signal (below −80 dBm): it may be outside your home.
high
An unknown device is online, or the network scan is failing.
medium
An unknown device joined in the last 24 hours and has left again.
low
The router integration is set up but cannot log in.
info
An optional feature is not set up.

This monitor sees which devices are on your network, not what they send. It cannot inspect traffic or see attacks from the internet.

Network scanner

About every 20 s the monitor pings every address in your /24 subnet and reads the ARP table. Change the pause between scans with PAUSA in monitor.py.

Telegram alerts

Get a message when a device you have never seen joins your network.

  1. In Telegram, talk to @BotFather, create a bot and copy its token.
  2. Send any message to your bot, open https://api.telegram.org/bot<token>/getUpdates and copy chat.id.
  3. Put both in config.json as telegram_token and telegram_chat_id.

Router integration

Optional. Shows the Wi-Fi band (2.4 / 5 GHz) and signal of each device, and keeps sleeping phones marked online.

Works with ZTE F680 routers. Put the router admin login in config.json as router_user and router_password. The monitor logs in for a second every minute, which may log you out of the router's own web page.

Privacy

  • The dashboard only answers on this computer (localhost).
  • Device data stays in dispositivos.json next to monitor.py.
  • Only the vendor half of each MAC address is sent to api.macvendors.com.
  • config.json and dispositivos.json are in .gitignore, so they never reach GitHub.
